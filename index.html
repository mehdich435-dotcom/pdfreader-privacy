Privacy Policy

App: PDF Reader: Scan & Edit (Android and iOS, package/bundle id com.folivo.app)
Last updated: November 1, 2026

1. Summary

All document processing happens on your device. We do not operate servers, we do not require an account, and your PDF files, scans and images are never uploaded by us anywhere.

2. Files and storage access

The app reads the PDF files and folders you choose (via the system file picker or folders you grant access to) in order to list, open, edit, merge, split, compress, protect and convert them. Results are saved only on your device, in locations you choose. Recent files and favourites are stored locally on your device.

3. Camera

The camera is used only when you start a document scan. Scanning and text recognition (OCR) run on-device (Google ML Kit on Android, Apple VisionKit/Vision on iOS). Captured images stay on your device.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect and process the advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA), IP address and device information (such as model, OS version and app interactions with ads) to deliver, measure and personalise ads. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

  • Consent (UMP): in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform is shown. You can change your choice anytime in Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • iOS App Tracking Transparency: on iOS, the IDFA is accessed only if you allow tracking in the ATT prompt. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

5. Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store, sell or share personal data. Apart from the ad SDK described above, the app does not use analytics.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; changes are posted on this page with a new date.
